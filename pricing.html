For workers

Workers pay nothing. Ever.

Claiming a profile, viewing records, collecting endorsements, replying, disputing and rehabilitating are all free for workers — by design.

If the people whose conduct is on record had to pay to see or contest it, the platform would not be fair, and it would not be lawful. Pricing falls entirely on the employer side that derives the commercial value.
