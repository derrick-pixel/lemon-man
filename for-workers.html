Your rights

Non-negotiable, and built into the architecture.

These are not promises in a policy document. They are constraints the platform enforces before a record can move.

✓

Notification before publication

You are told about any adverse record — its substance, its source, and how to respond — before any employer can see it.

✓

A 21-day right of reply

A full window to accept, add context, or dispute — matching the MAS reference-check standard. The record stays hidden throughout.

✓

Disputed means hidden

The moment you dispute a record it is hidden — not flagged — and stays hidden until an independent review concludes.

✓

Access & correction

Request everything on file at any time. Factual errors are corrected; expired records are purged on schedule.

Positive on record

Endorsements, not just the absence of marks.

Verified employers issue structured endorsements against fixed dimensions. Good work compounds into a score that opens doors — better shifts, faster placement, higher trust.

Punctuality

On time, every shift, across a deployment history.

Attendance

Shows up — and gives notice when life intervenes.

Customer handling

Trusted on the floor with clients and the public.

Data handling

Discreet with confidential and personal information.

If you disagree

Dispute it. Then rehabilitate.

Dispute with your own evidence

Upload your side — your roster, your messages, your medical documentation. The disputed record is pulled from view immediately and reviewed independently.

File a counter-record

Wage disputes, illegal deployment, retaliatory dismissal — workers can file structured records against employers, held to the same evidence standard. The record runs both ways.

Rehabilitate ahead of schedule

WSQ training and fresh endorsements feed a structured rehabilitation pathway that can accelerate the expiry of an upheld adverse record. A bad month is not a permanent sentence.

Always free for workers

Workers never pay to see, reply to, or dispute a record.

Claimed profiles, endorsements, disputes and rehabilitation are free — that is a design principle, not an introductory offer.
